Biography

Naliang Guo is an Assistant Research Fellow at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IGSNRR, CAS), and a member of Feng Wu's Lab. He holds a Ph.D. in Cartography and Geographic Information Systems and a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from China University of Geosciences, Wuhan. He undertook postdoctoral research at IGSNRR and passed his postdoctoral exit defense in October 2026.

His research focuses on geospatial intelligence through the integration of multimodal data and domain knowledge. He studies how remote sensing imagery, ground-based video, and text can be linked across space, time, and semantics to represent geographic events and support knowledge-guided analysis and reasoning. His work connects these methods with process simulation and decision support, with applications in human activity monitoring in ecologically important areas and urban disaster resilience.
